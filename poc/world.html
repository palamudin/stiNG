The whole world from one number: ground, water, forests, trails, rocks. Same seed, same world, on every machine. In the STING world it is the sim's seed.

Moves the sun. Its colour, the sky, the ambient light and the haze all come from one atmosphere model (single and multiple scattering), so a low sun reddens because the air took the blue. Exposure follows the light.

Distance to the point you look at, 6 cm to 9 km. Ground detail is refined round the camera; new detail blurs in as it arrives. The lens is a real thin lens, so close views get a macro depth of field.

Cuts mountains into ledges of 5 m: 0 is smooth slopes, 1 is sheer steps. Rebuilds the world. Hills and meadows are not stepped.

Aerosol in the air (Mie density) and a low mist that thins with height above the water. Changes how far you see and how the sun glows; does not change the clouds.

One wind for everything drawn: trees lean and rock, leaves flutter, grass bends in travelling gusts, waves grow. Presentation only; in the game the sim has its own wind.

The f-number of a 45 mm lens. Lower blurs more in front of and behind the focus. At landscape distances the blur is physically tiny at any setting.

Wets the ground (darker, glossier) and rings the water with drops. Falling rain and puddles are not drawn yet.

Drag to look round. Wheel or pinch to zoom from kilometres down to a wasp's view. WASD or arrows move, Shift is fast. Double-click to dive to a spot.
