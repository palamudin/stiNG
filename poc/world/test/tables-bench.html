Tables against live calculation

Two of the things a water pixel needs, done both ways over a full 1024 x 1024 pass, 20 passes each, timed on the GPU (EXT_disjoint_timer_query_webgl2; desktop Chrome and Edge give it, Firefox and Safari do not). Precision is measured against an exact reference computed on the CPU.

  • Glint count (water.js wPoisson): the Poisson draw by summing the series per pixel (up to 40 terms), against one texel read of the 512 x 512 table (rows: the mean on a square-root scale; columns: the uniform draw).
  • Shore distance: the distance to the water line by a live search (the clipmap's height against its water level on 48 taps along 16 directions at two radii), against one bilinear read of the shore table (shorefield.js, baked once by jump flooding).
